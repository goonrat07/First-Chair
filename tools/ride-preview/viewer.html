Tricks and events

    What the rides show

    1. Full-lock turns skid at almost any speed. With a turn rate of 1.8 rad/s, groomed grip only carves a full-lock turn below about 9 m/s, and a tuck reaches 29 m/s. The carving ride skids 23 to 55% of the time. Whether hard turns should carve at speed is a feel call for the playtest.
    2. The run-out doesn't stop a fast rider. A tucked run stands up on the flat and still leaves the map at 19 m/s. Braking stops the rider from 13 m/s in about 14 m. The slope needs a longer or uphill run-out.
    3. Grabs outscore spins. A 1 s Japan (778) is worth almost twice a clean 360 (420), because a grab earns up to 600 and a spin 100 per half turn. Those coefficients come from the old spec.
    4. A rail exit still scores as a small air. The drop off the rail's end is 0.33 s of air, above the 0.25 s placeholder minimum, so it scores 15 points.
    5. Flow on its own scores nothing. Seven near misses in the glade build Flow to 0.53, but Flow only multiplies later tricks.
    6. Unconfirmed: a weak launch on kicker 1. One scripted run lost speed on the lip while steering. A clean probe didn't reproduce it, so it needs a sim test.

    Every riding and scoring number is a placeholder until the first playtest on the PC. These rides are scripted by a simple steering controller, not a player.
