# Draft: what a player has, act by act

> **Status:** Draft for the user to choose from (sleep mode, 2026-11-02). It lays the three drafts side by side on
> one clock, if their recommended options are picked: the story (`story-outline.md`), the skis and gear
> (`gear-progression.md`) and the skill tree (`skill-tree.md`). Hours are estimates for a player who rides a bit of
> everything; nothing here is decided.

> **Replaced 2026-11-02 (`season-loop.md`):** the season is now laid out in days on a calendar, not in acts. Kept
> for its reasoning about pacing.

How the numbers were set:

- **Hours:** the story draft's estimate (prologue and act 1 about 2 hours; Bluebird aimed at 12 to 15 in all),
  spread by each act's mission count (7, 10, 9 and 9).
- **Skill points:** the skill tree's 40 (four branches leveling to 10), earned by riding at a steady rate, about
  3.3 an hour. A player who rides one style fills that branch first.
- **Skis:** each ski's earning rule from the gear draft, placed in the act where its rule first comes up (the
  Corduroy where the act 2 draft gives it, at Ilse's Cup). "Gold in every race" and "gold in every park event"
  depend on skill, so for many players they arrive an act later.

## What the timeline shows

- **Act 2 is busy, act 4 is thin.** Act 2 gives up to three skis (the Corduroy, the Ridge, often the Gate GS); act
  4 gives only the Birch Pro, and for many players later. Act 4 could give a festival ski of its own, or the Ridge
  could move to act 4 as the festival's prize.
- **The Pillow arrives late.** The powder ski comes in act 3, but powder is in act 1 (1-5, Bowie's ball) and act 2's
  black glades. Fine if powder should feel hard until the storm; otherwise earn it in act 2 (Wren's black glade).
- **The tree's moves outrun the riding layer.** By the end of act 2 a player has about 19 points, but the tree's
  moves (Stoke, Boost, Combos, Corks) are each their own build. If they don't exist yet, the points pile up with
  nothing to buy. So the skill tree should open at act 2, or the next riding layer should be built before story
  mode.
- **Gear and points come from different places,** gear from medals and missions, points from riding in a style, so
  a player who skips events still grows, and one who skips a style still gets the skis. This is the drafts working
  as intended.

## Decisions for the user

1. Is about 12 hours the right length for the story, or shorter (the prologue and act 1 first, about 2 hours)?
2. Even out the unlocks (a festival ski in act 4, or the Ridge as the festival's prize), or keep act 2 generous as
   the upper mountain's reward?
3. When should the skill tree open: from the prologue, or at act 2 once its first moves exist?
